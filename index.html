What is Pixl?

Centuries ago, Origin was the greatest digital civilization ever built, until the Great Static shattered it into islands lost in the Void. Its people crossed universes and found Hack Clubbers, who helped rebuild it under a new name: Pixl. A pixel-themed YSWS where every real project you ship repairs the world and unlocks real-world rewards.
