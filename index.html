What is Pixl?

Centuries ago, Origin was the greatest digital civilization ever built, until the Great Static shattered it into islands lost in the Void. Its people crossed universes and found Hack Clubbers, who helped rebuild it under a new name: Pixl. A pixel-themed YSWS where every real project you ship repairs the world and unlocks real-world rewards.

THE RESTORATION

How it works

Pixl runs as a story in seasons: chapters move the world forward, and everything you ship repairs it.

01

Join the world

Make a character and drop into Pixl, a 2D world you share with everyone else who is building right now.

Take a Trial

An NPC needs something real built: a website, an app, a piece of hardware. Pick one and it's yours.

Build your own thing

Or build something nobody asked for. Your own idea counts exactly the same.

02

Ship it

Send in what you built. A reviewer checks your hours and rates the build from Tier 1 to Tier 4.Harder build, higher tier.

03

It becomes Restoration Energy

Your hours multiplied by your tier. This one number is what everything else runs on.

T1 Spark 12.5 RE per hour
T2 Signal 15 RE per hour
T3 Grid 18.75 RE per hour
T4 Nexus 25 RE per hour
04

You get paid

Restoration Energy sets the rate that build pays. Every project starts at 40 pixels an hour and climbs toward 60 as it earns more energy, so bigger and harder builds pay more per hour. Pixels buy real things in the shop.

40
60

pixels per hour, per project

05

The world comes back

Your Restoration Energy also feeds the chapter goal that everyone fills together. Fill it and the story moves: a new region opens, NPCs return, new Trials appear.

Operations

Between chapters, one week themed events. A game jam, a hackathon, a week of building tools.

Joining late is fine

Unlocked regions never close. Turn up whenever and build anything that is open.
